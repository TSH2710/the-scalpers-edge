## The 12-Point Pre-Trade Checklist

Here's the exact checklist I use now. Every trade. No exceptions.

### Market Context (4 checks)

**1. What session is it?**
- First 30 min (9:30-10AM): High volatility, wider spreads. I reduce position size by 30%.
- Mid-session (10AM-12PM): My best window. Normal sizing.
- Lunch (12-1PM): Lower volume, choppier. I trade fewer setups.
- Power hour (3-4PM): High volatility again. Size down.

**2. Is there a news event in the next 30 minutes?**
- Fed speeches, CPI releases, earnings for my tickers — these are no-trade zones for me.
- I check the economic calendar the night before and again at 9:25AM.
- If there's a high-impact event, I sit out. Simple.

**3. What's the overall market doing?**
- SPY/QQQ above VWAP? Favor long setups.
- Below VWAP? Favor short setups.
- Chop? Don't trade. Just watch.

**4. Is my watchlist clean?**
- Maximum 5 tickers. No more.
- I set up my charts the night before. No adding new tickers at 9:35AM.
- Fewer tickers = better focus = better execution.

### Trade Setup (5 checks)

**5. Is there a clear setup?**
- Not "this looks like it might go up."
- A specific setup I trade: ORB breakout, VWAP pullback, momentum continuation, or support/resistance test.
- If I can't name the setup in one sentence, I don't take the trade.

**6. Is the risk/reward at least 1:1.5?**
- Minimum target: 1.5× my risk.
- If the setup only gives me 1:1 or worse, it's not worth it.
- This one filter eliminated 40% of my marginal trades.

**7. Do I have a defined stop loss?**
- Every trade. No exceptions.
- I know my exit BEFORE I enter.
- If I don't know where my stop is, I haven't done my homework.

**8. What's my position size?**
- Fixed risk per trade: 1% of my account.
- $5,000 account = $50 max risk per trade (1R).
- I calculate this before entry: (Risk per share) × (Shares) = $50 max.
- No mental math during the trade. Pre-calculated.

**9. What's my exit plan?**
- Two exits: the target (1.5-2R) and the stop (-1R).
- I also have a "trail stop" rule: if I'm up 1R, I move my stop to breakeven.
- Exit plan written before entry. Not decided during.

### Self-Check (3 checks)

**10. Am I in the right headspace?**
- Tired? Hungry? Angry from something earlier? Distracted?
- If any of those are yes, I trade half size or sit out entirely.
- I rate my mental state 1-10. Below a 7, I don't trade full size.

**11. Have I taken a loss recently?**
- 1 loss: normal. Keep going.
- 2 losses in a row: half size next trade.
- 3 losses: day over. Done. No exceptions.

**12. Why am I taking THIS trade?**
- Not "because it looks good." Not "because I need to make back yesterday's loss."
- Because it fits my setup criteria. Because the risk/reward is there. Because I've checked all 11 boxes above.
- One sentence: "I'm taking this trade because [specific reason]."
- If I can't fill in that blank, I don't take the trade.
