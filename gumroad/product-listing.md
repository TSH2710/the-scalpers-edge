12-point scalping-specific checklist: spread check, volume confirmation, news events, position sizing, stop loss confirmation, psychology check. Run through it before every session.

**3. R-Multiple Calculator**
Built-in Notion formulas so you see risk/reward at a glance. Track your average R-multiple — the only metric that predicts long-term profitability.

**4. Psychology Tracker**
FOMO flags, revenge trading indicators, overconfidence checkboxes, mood sliders. Log your emotional state after every trade and start seeing patterns.

**5. Weekly Review Dashboard**
Auto-aggregated stats: win rate, average R, best/worst setups, time-of-day performance, consecutive win/loss streaks, total commissions, total spread costs.

**6. Wash Sale Tracker**
Calendar-based tracker for tax loss harvesting concerns. Never accidentally trigger a wash sale again.

**7. Scalp-Specific Metrics**
Average hold time, spread cost tracking, win rate by session (first 30 min, 10AM-11AM, lunch, power hour, last 30 min). Built for speed.
