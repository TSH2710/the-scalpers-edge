Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Scalper's Edge ("we", "us") collects when you visit thescalpersedge.com or buy the Disciplined Trader Journal, and what we do with it. Plain English, no legalese where we can avoid it.

What we collect

  • Email address — if you request the free lite version of the journal, you give us your email so we can send it to you.
  • Purchase information — payments are processed by Gumroad. We never see or store your card details; we receive your email address and purchase status from Gumroad so we can support your purchase.
  • Form submissions — forms on this site are handled by Netlify, which stores the submitted data (such as your email) on our behalf and forwards it to us.
  • Basic usage data — like most websites, our host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance purposes.

What we do with it

  • Send you the free lite template you requested.
  • Send occasional trading tips or product updates. Every email includes an unsubscribe link — one click, no hard feelings.
  • Support your purchase and process refunds under the 14-day guarantee.
  • Keep the site secure and prevent spam (we use a hidden honeypot field to filter bots).

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address or personal data. Ever.
  • We do not see, store, or have access to your payment card information.
  • We do not know anything about your actual trades — the journal is a Notion template that lives in your own Notion workspace. Your trading data never touches our systems.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of processors to run this business:

  • Netlify — hosts this website and handles form submissions (privacy: netlify.com/privacy).
  • Gumroad — processes payments and product delivery (privacy: gumroad.com/privacy).
  • Google/Gmail — receives form notification emails so we can respond to you.

These services process data only as needed to provide their function. Their own privacy policies apply to their handling of your information.

Data retention and your rights

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. To access, correct, export, or delete any personal data we hold about you, email us and we will take care of it promptly.

Not financial advice

Content on this site is educational only and reflects personal experience. Nothing here is financial, investment, or tax advice. Consult a licensed professional before making trading decisions.
