## Ongoing Content Templates

### Daily Posts (rotate these)

**Discipline tip:**
> Scalping discipline tip [X]: [specific tip]
>
> Example: "Scalping discipline tip: Never increase position size after a win. One bad trade wipes out three good ones. Stick to your risk per trade. Every. Single. Time."

**Journal insight:**
> Just journaled [X] trades. Found [insight].
>
> Example: "Just journaled 12 trades. Found that my win rate is 68% in the first 30 min, but only 31% after 2PM. Time to close earlier."

**Poll:**
> Quick poll for scalpers:
>
> [Question]
> A) [Option]
> B) [Option]
> C) [Option]
>
> Examples:
> "Quick poll: How many trades do you take per day?"
> "Quick poll: What's your biggest struggle? A) Psychology B) Risk management C) Finding setups D) Sticking to a plan"

**Mistake callout:**
> Common scalping mistake: [specific mistake]
>
> Example: "Common scalping mistake: Chasing a move that already ran. If you missed the entry, wait for the pullback. FOMO entries lose 70% of the time. Journal it and you'll see."

**R-multiple education:**
> R-multiple reminder: [specific concept]
>
> Example: "R-multiple reminder: A 1:1 risk/reward with a 60% win rate = profitable. A 2:1 R:R with a 40% win rate = more profitable. But you'll never know your win rate if you don't journal."

**Template showcase:**

## Growth Strategy

1. **Reply to big accounts** — Find 5-10 trading accounts with 10K+ followers. Reply thoughtfully to their posts daily. Their audience becomes your audience.
2. **Threads perform** — Write one good thread per week. "How I went from losing $2K/month to consistent in 3 months" type content.
3. **Screenshots work** — Post screenshots of your journal entries (anonymized). Visual proof beats text.
4. **Be consistent** — 2-3 posts/day, every day. The algorithm rewards consistency.
