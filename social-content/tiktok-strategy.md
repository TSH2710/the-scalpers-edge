# TikTok Content Strategy — @scalpersedge

## Platform Overview

TikTok is the best short-form platform for trading content because:
- Algorithm favors educational content
- Trading is naturally visual (charts, P&L screenshots)
- 60-second format = perfect for quick tips and mindset content
- Cross-post to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels

## Video Types (Rotate These)

### Type 1: "Mistake Callout" (Highest Engagement)

**Format:** Face-to-camera + text overlay + chart graphic
**Length:** 15-30 seconds
**Hook (first 3 seconds):** State the mistake directly

**Script Template:**
```
[0-3s] HOOK: "If you're still doing this, you're bleeding money."
[3-10s] EXPLAIN: "X% of scalpers [specific mistake]. Here's why it kills you."
[10-20s] EVIDENCE: "[Specific data or example — e.g., 'I tracked 30 trades — revenge trades averaged -2.1R']"
[20-30s] FIX: "Instead, do [specific actionable fix]."
[30s] CTA: "Follow for more scalping discipline."
```

**Examples:**
1. "If you revenge trade after a loss, you're not scalping — you're gambling."
2. "Checking your P&L every 10 seconds during a trade is costing you money."
3. "Trading more shares to 'make it back' is the #1 way to blow up your account."

---

### Type 2: "Journal With Me" (Builds Trust)

**Format:** Screen recording of Notion template + voiceover
**Length:** 30-60 seconds
**Hook:** "Come journal with me"

**Script Template:**
```
[0-3s] HOOK: "This is how I journal every single trade."
[3-15s] SHOW: Walk through filling out one trade in the template
[15-30s] HIGHLIGHT: Point out a specific feature (R-multiple calculator, psychology tracker)
[30-45s] RESULT: Show the weekly stats auto-populating
[45-60s] INSIGHT: "Last week my data showed I lose 70% after 2PM. So now I stop at 1:30."
```

**Examples:**
1. "How I fill out my trade log in 2 minutes flat"
2. "The one feature in my journal that changed my trading"
3. "My weekly review process — and what it revealed about my worst trading day"

---

### Type 3: "Quick Tip" (High Shareability)

**Format:** Static text on chart background + voiceover
**Length:** 10-20 seconds
**Hook:** Quick, punchy statement

**Script Template:**
```
[0-3s] HOOK: "[Controversial or surprising statement]"
[3-10s] EXPLAIN: "[One sentence explanation]"
[10-15s] PROOF: "[One data point or example]"
```

**Examples:**
1. "Your win rate doesn't matter. Your R-multiple does. Track it."
2. "If your spread costs are over 15% of gross profit, you're trading the wrong stocks."
3. "3 revenge trades in a week = mandatory day off. Non-negotiable."
4. "The best time to stop trading is BEFORE you start losing. Not after."
5. "Journaling 20 trades takes less time than one losing trade you could've avoided."

---

### Type 4: "Myth Busting" (Contrarian = Engagement)

**Format:** Text overlay + voiceover + reaction
**Length:** 20-40 seconds
**Hook:** Challenge a common belief

**Script Template:**
```
[0-3s] HOOK: "Unpopular opinion: [counter-intuitive statement]"
[3-15s] ARGUE: "Everyone says [common belief]. But [data/reasoning]."
[15-30s] REVEAL: "The truth is [your take]."
[30-40s] CTA: "What's your take? Comment below."
```

**Examples:**
1. "Unpopular opinion: You don't need a 60% win rate to be profitable. My average is 43% and I'm green every month."
2. "Day trading gurus won't tell you this: journaling is more profitable than any $500 course."
3. "Taking 3 trades a day > 15 trades a day. Quality over quantity."
4. "You don't need to be in the market every day. The best traders I know take 20+ days off per year."

---

### Type 5: "Chart Breakdown" (Authority Building)

**Format:** Chart analysis with voiceover
**Length:** 45-60 seconds
**Hook:** "Let me show you what I see here"

**Script Template:**
```
[0-3s] HOOK: "Here's a trade I took today and why."
[3-20s] ANALYSIS: Walk through the setup — levels, volume, why you entered
[20-35s] EXECUTION: Entry, stop, target, what actually happened
[35-50s] RESULT: Win or loss, what you learned
[50-60s] JOURNAL: "This is exactly what goes in my journal — setup, reasoning, outcome."
```

**Note:** Anonymize tickers and P&L if you want to keep it private. Use "Stock A" instead of actual ticker.

## Higgsfield Workflow

**For each video:**

1. **Write the script** (use templates above) — 2 min
2. **Generate via Higgsfield:**
   - Avatar: Professional but approachable (trader aesthetic)
   - Voice: Clear, confident, slightly conversational
   - Background: Clean desk setup with trading monitor or dark background
   - Text overlays: Key stats, quotes, CTAs
3. **Edit in CapCut** (free):
   - Add captions (auto-generate in CapCut)
   - Add background music (low, instrumental)
   - Trim to optimal length for each platform
   - Add end screen with handle and website
4. **Schedule** — post 3x/week minimum, best times: 7AM, 12PM, 6PM ET

## Cross-Posting Strategy

**TikTok** → Post first
**YouTube Shorts** → Same video, different title/description (1-2 days later)
**Instagram Reels** → Same video, crop to 9:16 (1-2 days later)
**Twitter** → Clip the best 30 seconds, post as native video

Each platform gets slightly different:
- **TikTok:** Trendy audio, fast cuts, emoji-heavy captions
- **YouTube Shorts:** More detailed captions, links in description
- **Instagram:** More visual, use Stories to drive to Reel
- **Twitter:** Text-heavy captions, thread context

## Growth Hacks

1. **Reply to trading creators** — Comment on their videos with real insight. Their viewers become yours.
2. **Duet/stitch popular trading videos** — Add your take on a popular take. Controversy = views.
3. **Series content** — "30 Days of Journaling" series. People follow along.
4. **Before/after** — "What my trading looked like before journaling vs after"
5. **Numbers content** — "I journaled 50 trades. Here's exactly what I found." Numbers = trust.
