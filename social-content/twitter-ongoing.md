## Mistake Callouts

53. Common scalping mistake: Entering without a stop loss. "I'll just watch it" is not a risk management strategy.

54. Common scalping mistake: Increasing position size to make back a loss. This is how accounts blow up. Stick to your risk per trade.

55. Common scalping mistake: Chasing a move that already ran. If you missed the entry, wait for the pullback. FOMO entries lose 70% of the time.

56. Common scalping mistake: Trading the last 30 minutes of the day. Liquidity drops, spreads widen, and your edge disappears.

57. Common scalping mistake: Averaging down on a losing position. If you were wrong, admit it. Adding more shares doesn't make you right.

58. Common scalping mistake: Trading through lunch. Your focus is garbage. The best setups aren't there anyway.

59. Common scalping mistake: Ignoring spread costs. A $0.02 spread on 500 shares = $10 per trade. In and out = $20. Do that 10 times a day = $200/day.

60. Common scalping mistake: Checking Twitter during a trade. If you need distraction, your position is too big.

## R-Multiple Education

61. R-multiple 101: If you risk $100 per trade and make $200, that's a 2R. If you risk $100 and make $50, that's 0.5R. Track this number.

62. R-multiple truth: You can be profitable with a 35% win rate if your average R is above 1.5. You can lose money with a 70% win rate if your average R is below 0.5.

63. R-multiple reminder: After 20 trades, calculate your average R. If it's above 1.0, your strategy works. Don't change anything.

64. R-multiple comparison: Strategy A wins 60% of the time at 0.8R average. Strategy B wins 40% at 1.4R average. B is more profitable. Follow B.

65. R-multiple mistake: Taking profits too early. If your target is 2R and you exit at 0.5R because you're scared, your average R suffers. Set targets. Hit them.
