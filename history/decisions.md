# Key Decisions

Documenting important decisions and the reasoning behind them.

## Content Strategy

### Decision: Honest content only (no fabricated P&L numbers)
**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Reasoning:** Traders check receipts. One screenshot request you can't answer ends credibility permanently. Teaching content persuades better than fake memoir.
**Impact:** All blog posts rewritten, testimonials replaced with honest quotes

### Decision: Free Lite as lead magnet
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Reasoning:** Free gets people in the door. Cross-sell at checkout + email sequence converts them to paid. Lower barrier to entry.
**Impact:** Created Lite product, set up cross-sell, drafted email sequence

### Decision: One-time payment ($49) vs subscription
**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Reasoning:** Traders hate recurring costs. One-time payment with lifetime updates is more appealing and builds trust.
**Impact:** Simple pricing, no subscription complexity

## Social Strategy

### Decision: Replies > Posts at 0 followers
**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Reasoning:** Nobody sees posts with no audience. Smart replies under big trading accounts get discovered. Profile link does the conversion work.
**Impact:** Focus on 5-10 useful replies/day, not just posting

### Decision: No buying followers, no follow/unfollow
**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Reasoning:** Trading community screenshots fakes. Honest positioning IS the differentiation. Built to last, not to inflate numbers.
**Impact:** Organic growth only, authentic engagement

### Decision: Native video uploads only on X
**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Reasoning:** X buries outbound video links. Native uploads get shown to more people.
**Impact:** Record videos, upload directly to X

## Technical

### Decision: Netlify for hosting
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Reasoning:** Free tier, auto-deploy on push, good performance, easy DNS setup when domain is registered.
**Impact:** Site auto-deploys on every push to main

### Decision: OpenCLI for social automation
**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Reasoning:** Already installed, supports X and Instagram, can automate posting without manual work.
**Impact:** Scheduled posts, automated content distribution

### Decision: Pushover for notifications
**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Reasoning:** Free, reliable push notifications to phone. Simple setup, no complex integrations.
**Impact:** Get notified when automated posts go live

## Pricing

### Decision: LAUNCH20 capped at 25 uses
**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Reasoning:** Honest scarcity. Cap is real, set in Gumroad. Creates urgency without being fake.
**Impact:** 20% off for first 25 buyers, then full price

### Decision: 14-day refund policy
**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Reasoning:** Removes risk for buyers. "No hoops" policy builds trust. Most won't refund if product delivers value.
**Impact:** Set on both products, mentioned everywhere

## What We Didn't Do

### Decision: No fake testimonials
**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Reasoning:** Can't verify, easily disproven, damages credibility if exposed.
**Impact:** Replaced with honest creator quote and problem statement

### Decision: No "50+ traders" social proof
**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Reasoning:** Invented number. Real social proof takes time to build.
**Impact:** Replaced with true claims (one-time payment, lifetime updates, 14-day guarantee)
