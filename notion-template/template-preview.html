Pre-Trade Checklist
Run through these 12 checks before every single trade. Takes 90 seconds. Saves hours of losses.
⏱️
The Rule: Don't enter a single trade until every box below is checked. No exceptions. No "just this once." Every. Single. Trade.
Market Context
  • ✓Session check: What session is it? First 30 min (size -30%), mid-session (normal), lunch (selective), power hour (size -20%)
  • ✓News events: Checked economic calendar. No high-impact events (Fed, CPI, earnings) in the next 30 minutes?
  • ✓Market bias: SPY/QQQ above VWAP → favor long. Below VWAP → favor short. Chop → don't trade.
  • ✓Watchlist clean: Max 5 tickers. Set up charts night before. No adding tickers at 9:35AM.
Trade Setup
  • ✓Named setup: Can I name the setup in one sentence? (ORB breakout, VWAP pullback, momentum continuation, support/resistance test)
  • ✓Risk/reward: Is the target at least 1.5× my risk? Minimum 1:1.5 R:R ratio.
  • ✓Stop loss defined: I know my exit price BEFORE I enter. Not decided during the trade.
  • ✓Position sized: Calculated 1% account risk. (Account size × 0.01) ÷ (Entry - Stop) = share count.
  • ✓Exit plan: Target (1.5-2R), stop (-1R), and trail stop rule (move to breakeven at +1R) — all written before entry.
Self-Check
  • ✓Headspace: Rated my mental state 7+/10. Tired, hungry, angry, or distracted → half size or sit out.
  • ✓Loss streak check: 1 loss = normal. 2 losses = half size. 3 losses = day over. No exceptions.
  • ✓One-sentence reason: "I'm taking this trade because [specific setup/setup name at specific level]." If I can't fill this in, I don't take it.
📐
Position Size Formula: (Account × 0.01) ÷ (Entry - Stop) = Shares
Example: $5,000 account, $0.20 risk/share → (5000 × 0.01) ÷ 0.20 = 250 shares

R-Multiple Calculator
Track R-multiples to know if your strategy is actually profitable. Win rate is a distraction. R is the truth.
📐
The Formula: R = (Exit - Entry) / (Entry - Stop)
Example: Entry $100, Stop $98, Exit $104 → R = ($104 - $100) / ($100 - $98) = 2R
Benchmark: Average R above 1.0 = strategy is profitable. Below 0.8 = exits need work.

Wash Sale Rule: Can't claim a loss if you buy "substantially identical" security within 30 days before/after the sale. The disallowed loss gets added to your cost basis.
